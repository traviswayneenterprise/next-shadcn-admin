# Phase 1 Weekly Focus Guides

These guides turn the [six-week delivery plan](phase-1-six-week-plan.md) into weekly execution checklists. The six delivery weeks run from August 17 through September 27, 2026. Week 7 is contingency only.

Named ownership and daily deliverables are defined in the [Travis and Mr. Miracle task-assignment matrix](task-assignments-travis-miracle.md).

| Week | Focus | Dates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Week 1](week-01-foundation-contract-design-system.md) | Foundation, contract, and design system | Aug 17–23 |
| [Week 2](week-02-catalogue-commerce-access.md) | Catalogue, commerce, and learner access | Aug 24–30 |
| [Week 3](week-03-content-import-lessons.md) | Content, curriculum import, and lessons | Aug 31–Sep 6 |
| [Week 4](week-04-assessments-submissions-reviews.md) | Assessments, submissions, and reviews | Sep 7–13 |
| [Week 5](week-05-cohorts-notifications.md) | Cohorts and notifications | Sep 14–20 |
| [Week 6](week-06-certificates-hardening-beta.md) | Certificates, hardening, and beta | Sep 21–27 |
| [Week 7](week-07-contingency.md) | Contingency and remediation | Sep 28–Oct 4 |

## How to use each guide

1. Confirm its scope and assignments during Monday planning.
2. Copy its deliverables into cross-repository issues.
3. Assign one implementer and one reviewer to every issue.
4. Record deviations in the Friday review; do not silently change the weekly gate.
5. Carry unfinished critical work forward by explicitly displacing lower-priority work.

The product owner retains final approval authority for protected decisions.
