# Domain Glossary

- **Track**: certifiable learning path containing ordered courses.
- **Course**: purchasable-capable subdivision of a track; disabled as an
  individual offering in Phase 1.
- **Module**: ordered instructional grouping inside a course.
- **Lesson**: stable identity for an instructional unit.
- **Lesson version**: immutable published or editable draft representation.
- **Offering**: commercial configuration granting access to a track or course.
- **Price**: immutable currency and integer-subunit amount for an offering.
- **Entitlement**: effective right to access a target, derived from payment or a
  manual grant.
- **Enrollment**: learner's learning-state relationship to a track.
- **Cohort**: managed delivery group with capacity, pacing, sessions, and dates.
- **Mastery**: satisfaction of configured quiz or prerequisite rules.
- **Submission**: stable assignment response with append-only versions.
- **Review**: rubric evaluation of one submission version.
- **Certificate eligibility**: satisfied requirements awaiting final approval.
- **Certificate**: approved, verifiable track credential; not accreditation.
